# Grand Portage core specification — G1 proposal
GP holds only claims justified by replayable evidence and sound admitted rules, with the statement, model, assumptions and versions bound explicitly. Every held claim is true in every context its scope denotes, under the admitted checker/rule contracts. It also exposes consequences reachable within a finite specified closure domain. It does not establish the intended meaning of a wrongly specified problem.

The rational/exact workbench serves finite, algebraic and real-polynomial questions; arithmetic negatives remain partial. Available theory is not implemented checker completeness. Search, citations and assertions stay visible without authority.

**Types.** A profile supplies statements (including selected points/regions), contexts, finite scope descriptions with semantics, a decidable sound scope-inclusion test and a sound declared-contradiction test. A claim is statement+scope. Multiple immutable warrant records support a claim: checked receipts or bound Lean proofs, admitted derivations with premise-warrant IDs, citations, assertions and absent attempts. Bindings cover statement/scope/model data, input digests and admitted checker/rule identities and versions. The kernel compares identities; adapters compute bytes/digests under explicit contracts.

**Held.** At the final folded snapshot, a claim is held iff at least one active warrant has exact current bindings, sufficient reach and an acyclic live derivation through active supporting warrants. A failed retry does not revoke an independent success. Explicit retraction targets a warrant; dependency invalidation follows that support, while independent support survives. Supersession changes the current input/version identity; it retains historical evidence. Proposed semantics require corpus scoring before G1 sign-off.

**K1–K6.** K1 admits only validated receipts/proofs from registered sound contracts. K2 narrows universally quantified scope using the profile inclusion test; it never broadens reach. K3 applies only registered sound derivations with live premises and checked side conditions. K4 invalidates mismatched bindings. K5 excludes cited/asserted/absent evidence from authority. K6 uses an append-only, versioned typed log and deterministic total fold; merge is ordered concatenation plus re-fold. Source conflict/order remains explicit. Promotion closes only a declared finite materialization domain; unsupported transports remain obligations.

**Inconsistency.** Active contradictory statements at provably overlapping scopes freeze further promotion and report their supporting warrants. Unknown overlap yields an unresolved finding; it does not assert consistency or entailment.

**Boundaries.** The Mathlib-free kernel owns custody and admitted-rule closure; profiles/checkers own domain semantics. Selected roots/points and geometric regions belong in statements/models; field properties, characteristic and quantified assumptions belong in scopes. A08b's arbitrary nontrivial-ring identity versus A05's characteristic-two coefficient obstruction supports this split; DK objective/model incidents demonstrate why custody cannot supply intent.

**G1 adoption proposals.** Support both bound Lean proofs and admitted receipts through one scope interface; imitate MathEvidence's capability/binding discipline first, adopting exact components only after fit checks; use pinned Mathlib vocabulary through a separate sound binding layer. These three decisions require Will's sign-off. Native LRAT's runtime/compiler assumptions remain explicit; no checker is admitted by this spec.
